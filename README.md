## Overview

_A social horror mod that makes you feel alone among friends._

This is not a mod about monsters.
When you and your friends join a server, they vanish from your world—no nametags, no skins, no visible trace. But you can hear them: footsteps, mining, whispers… they're nearby, yet you can never pinpoint their location.
Until one day, you start seeing **"them"**—figures that look exactly like your friends stand in the distance, staring. When you approach, they disappear. But they're still there, in the corner you can't see, waiting with your friends' faces.
You begin to wonder: was that voice really your friend? Who is that figure in the distance?

## Innovative Mechanics


Social Invisibility: Players are completely invisible to each other by default—only sound reveals presence. True "invisible multiplayer" turns trust into a luxury.

Dynamic Stress System: Your actions and surroundings accumulate "stress"—darkness and isolation add to it, and glimpsing things that shouldn't exist adds even more. High stress distorts your vision, thickens the air, and triggers psychological whispers.

Mimic Ghosts: The server spawns ghosts that copy other players' skins and names, targeting specific victims. They extinguish light sources, teleport, and even play voice snippets of the players they mimic (with Simple Voice Chat), blurring the line between friend and foe.

Brief Reveals: Only under certain conditions (like being close during daytime) do players become temporarily visible. In those seconds, what will you do?


## Critical Info


Multiplayer only: Single-player disables core features.

Integrates with Simple Voice Chat—ghosts can play voice snippets of mimicked players (requires mod on both sides).

Beta stage: The mod is currently in active development. If you encounter any bugs or technical issues, please report them on GitHub (do not report intended gameplay mechanics as issues). Suggestions and ideas are also welcome!

## Game-Changing Modifications

This mod fundamentally alters vanilla multiplayer:

Players are completely invisible, intangible, and unable to interact with each other by default—only sound gives away their presence.
Ghosts can extinguish torches, lanterns, and other light sources, making safe zones unreliable.

Eating certain foods (rotten flesh vs. cooked meat) affects your stress, changing how you manage hunger.

Attacking animals, villagers, or other players increases stress, while killing monsters reduces it—combat choices now have psychological consequences.
High stress triggers visual and auditory hallucinations (fog, particles, whispered messages), impairing your perception.

The Recorder item adds stress when used, with a cooldown—using it is a risk.
Player visibility is only temporary under specific conditions, breaking the usual cooperative dynamic.
